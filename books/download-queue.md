## How Chais downloads these

**Route 1 — official bulk export (recommended, do it first):** Meta AI app →
Settings → Data & privacy → Manage your information → Download your
information → Export. Up to 30 days to prepare, 4-day download window.
Unverified whether artifact contents are inside the export — check it when it lands.

**Route 2 — hand-download per title:** open meta.ai/artifacts while signed in,
open the title, download/save it. Slow but certain.

**Route 3 — WhatsApp:** send `/download-all-ai-info` in WhatsApp.

**Route 4 — bulk video:** a third-party downloader extension for bulk video
artifacts (only if Routes 1–3 fail).

**Where the files go:** hand them to Muse (upload here, or drop into
`~/workspace/user/files/`). Muse ingests each file into the Vault Library,
dedupes it against the 35 duplicated titles, and gives it a per-title release
record. A title is NOT "in hand" until its file is on disk here.
